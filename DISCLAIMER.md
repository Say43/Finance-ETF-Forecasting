- All results are **historical backtests**. Past performance does not predict
  future results. Backtests are idealised: they cannot fully capture slippage,
  liquidity, market impact, borrowing costs, taxes, or the behaviour of a real
  investor under stress.
- The headline finding of this project is **negative**: the fine-tuned
  forecasting model has **no measurable directional edge** on liquid ETFs, and
  trading on its signal underperformed buy-and-hold — with and without fees.
  See [docs/results.md](docs/results.md). Any use of this code to trade real
  money would be contrary to its own findings.
- The portfolio results that *are* positive (trend + volatility scaling)
